Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register an account, browse our catalog, or place an order, we collect essential personal information including your full name, email address, shipping destination, and order history.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your personal data is used solely to process orders, communicate shipment tracking updates, manage user authentication, and improve storefront performance. We do not sell or rent customer data to third-party advertisers.

3. Payment Security & Encryption

All sensitive transactions are encrypted via industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols. Passwords are cryptographically hashed using salted bcrypt algorithms and are never stored in plain text.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We utilize local client-side storage to preserve your active shopping cart items and maintain secure session tokens across page visits. You may clear your browser cookies at any time.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You maintain the right to inspect the personal data associated with your account, correct inaccurate information, or request permanent deletion of your profile by reaching out to our privacy compliance desk.
